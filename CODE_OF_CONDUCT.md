# Code of Conduct

We want MCP Studio to be a welcoming project for everyone, regardless of experience, background, or
identity.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that helps the community:

- Being kind, patient, and respectful, especially with newcomers.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and the community.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks in any form.
- Discriminatory jokes or language.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or other conduct that is inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when someone
represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and other contributions that do
not follow this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behavior they consider
inappropriate. Report problems privately to the maintainers by opening a confidential security
advisory on the repository or by contacting the organization owners.

Maintainers will review every report promptly and fairly, and will respect the privacy of the
reporter.
